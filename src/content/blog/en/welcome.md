This is the first post on my blog, let me explain what it is and why it
exists.

## Why a blog?

I started this blog to share what I learn and experiment with.
It is a way for me to compel myself to slow down and organize my thoughts.
I'll do my best to try to being clear and easy to understand.
As I am clearly not the best writer, this blog is an opportunity for me to improve.
So please bear with me (or not) if you are reading! :)
Some of my posts might be AI-generated from my personal notes.
When that is the case, it will be clearly stated.

## What to expect

- Short posts about some of my activities and experiments
- Notes on what I learn and discover in this new age of AI and LLMs
- Occasional thoughts on life in general, and my own in particular

No promises about a schedule or content.
